## What is OpenAgent?

OpenAgent is a self-hosted platform built around three core capabilities:

**Intelligent Agents** — Agents powered by any supported LLM that can reason, use tools via MCP, search your knowledge base, and call external APIs to complete complex tasks.

**Knowledge Base** — Upload documents (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, and more). OpenAgent chunks, embeds, and indexes them so agents can retrieve precise, grounded answers.

**Multi-Channel Gateway** — One deployment reaches your users wherever they are: Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, web chat, and more.

## Why OpenAgent?

- **Open source** — Apache 2.0 licensed. Self-host on your own infrastructure.
- **Model agnostic** — Switch between providers without changing your agent logic.
- **Privacy first** — Your data never leaves your servers.
- **Extensible** — Add custom tools, channels, and model providers via plugins.
- **Enterprise features** — SSO, RBAC, multi-tenancy, and audit logs built in.
